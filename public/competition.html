SYNQ

Say where you need to be. The city moves you.

SYNQ is a mobility operating system for Greater Colombo in 2100. It is a Round 01 frontend prototype: no backend, no tickets, no live vehicle APIs.

Concept

In 2100, Colombo’s network is too large for people to assemble by hand: shared pods, coastal rail, scarce air shuttles, and flood-aware roads. A traveller states a destination, a time, and what matters. SYNQ returns two or three explainable journeys. A 3D twin shows the network as infrastructure. When rain closes Coastal Road 04, SYNQ moves the traveller and says so in plain language.

Required screens

  • Home / discovery — intent planner on the city twin
  • Journey details — legs, why, alternatives, start
  • Live tracking — spatial twin, ETA, rain reroute, arrival

Demo
