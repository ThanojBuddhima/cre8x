# SYNQ — Cre8X 3.0 Round 01

**Product:** SYNQ, a mobility operating system for Greater Colombo in 2100  
**Tagline:** Say where you need to be. The city moves you.  
**Theme:** IMAGINE 2100 — Design what comes next  
**Domain:** Transportation

## Concept (100 words)

In 2100, Colombo’s network is too large for people to assemble by hand: shared pods, coastal rail, scarce air shuttles, and flood-aware roads. SYNQ is not a booking app. A traveller states a destination, a time, and what matters — fastest, calm, accessible, or low energy. The city’s mobility intelligence returns two or three explainable journeys. A 3D twin shows the network as infrastructure, not decoration. When rain closes Coastal Road 04, SYNQ moves the traveller and says so in plain language. People should not have to understand transportation. They should only have to say where they need to be.

## World model

Documented trends we extrapolate from: Mobility-as-a-Service, autonomous shared fleets, digital twins, NASA-style urban air mobility as a limited mode, and IPCC-range sea-level / monsoon flood risk for low-lying Colombo.

Fictional product assumptions (design fiction, not a forecast): private cars are rare inside the metro; humans set intent; the network assigns vehicles; air is used when time or flood risk justifies the energy; a manual/calm path always exists.

## Core screens

1. **Home / Mobility Command** — Intent card on the city twin. From, to, arrive-by, what matters. No mode checkboxes.
2. **Journey details** — Arrival time, timeline of legs, why this journey, energy / weather / transfers, alternatives, Start.
3. **Live tracking** — Twin or 2D schematic, now/next/ETA, layer toggles, disruption banner, quiet arrival.

## Demo

Colombo Fort Hub → KDU, arrive 08:30. Rain at live 40%. Reroute to the accessible rail journey. Arrival 08:27. Copy: “I moved you off a flood-risk road.”

## Judging

- Usability: one intent, few options, always a next action  
- Aesthetics: civic night infrastructure, not neon  
- Innovation: intent + twin + climate explanation  
- Accessibility: Calm mode, 0-stair option, reduced motion  
- Functionality: complete loop with disruption  
- Mobile: bottom-first cards, simplified 3D or 2D fallback  

## What we did not build

Auth, payments, tickets, databases, live AI APIs, real GIS, fleet IoT.
